Common questions

How do I add my child's word list from school?

Open Grown-ups (answer the quick grown-ups question), then Word lists → New word list. Type or paste the words, or tap Take a photo of a list. For the best photo, crop it so only the words are inside the corners, lay the paper flat in good light, and check the words before tapping Add. Taking a photo is part of the Full Version.

How do I record my own voice?

In Grown-ups, tap Record your voice, or open a word list and tap Record your voice for these words. Record each word once and it's used in every game and every list. If the app says the microphone is off, turn it on in the iPad's Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

When does a word count as "known"?

When your child gets it right on three different days. This stops a lucky guess from counting and shows which words have really stuck. You can see every word's progress in Grown-ups → Progress.

What is "Listen for my child"?

An optional Flash Cards setting. When it's on, the app listens for your child reading the word out loud and moves on when it hears it. It may miss some words, especially short ones or young voices. It never marks a word wrong, and you can always swipe or tap. Listening happens only on the device. The audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. It's off until you turn it on in Grown-ups → Flash Cards.

I can't hear the words.

Turn up the volume with the buttons on the side of the iPad. The words play even when the mute switch is on. If you use headphones or a speaker, check that it's connected.

I bought the Full Version. How do I get it on another device?

Sign in to the same Apple Account, open Grown-ups → Full Version, and tap Restore purchase. With Family Sharing turned on, everyone in your family can use it too.

Can I move my child's progress to a new device?

Profiles, word lists, recordings and progress are saved only on the device they were made on. There's no account. If you set up a new iPad from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, they usually come along with the backup.

How do I add another child?

Grown-ups → tap the child's name at the top → Add a kid. Each child gets their own friend, word list and progress. More than one profile is part of the Full Version.
